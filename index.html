Better Facilities.

A student complaint and maintenance management portal for NMIMS Shirpur — report issues, track progress, and see resolutions in real time.

Login & Report Explore Features

Everything You Need

Streamlined workflow from complaint to resolution.

📋

Easy Complaint Submission

Submit issues across any campus facility — labs, hostels, library, sports complex — with category, location, and priority.

📡

Real-Time Status Tracking

Each complaint gets a unique Ticket ID. Follow every update through a clear timeline from submission to resolution.

🔧

Staff Work Queue

Maintenance staff view assigned tasks, update work status, and leave technician notes for full traceability.

🛡️

Admin Control Panel

Administrators triage new complaints, assign staff, change priorities, view summary counts and export complaints to CSV.

🔍

Smart Filtering

Filter complaints by status, category, priority, or keyword. Results update instantly — no page reloads.

🌙

Light & Dark Mode

Switch between light and dark themes with one click. Preference is saved to your browser automatically.

How It Works

Four simple steps from problem to solution.

1

Login

Sign in with your SAP ID to access your personal dashboard.

2

Report

Describe the issue, select the location, category and priority.

3

Track

View your ticket ID and live status updates in your dashboard.

4

Resolved

See the status change to Resolved on your dashboard.
